# Nano-Interface-Ready Neuro-Semantic State Communication

## Abstract

Building on our published WITCOM 2025 NDAN chapter, this work introduces a new neuro-semantic state communication layer for privacy-preserving human-AI cognitive networks. Instead of transmitting raw neural signals or presenting an untestable telepathic claim, the proposed protocol communicates a task-relevant state estimate with uncertainty, consent scope, and provenance. The architecture connects non-invasive BCI observations, cognitive-state estimation, adaptive networking, and governance policies. Nanotechnology is treated as a future sensing substrate involving flexible electrodes, nanosensors, and low-power edge electronics; no fabricated nanodevice or clinical result is claimed. The presentation covers the protocol model, safety constraints, validation metrics, and a roadmap for human-in-the-loop experiments.

**Keywords:** neurodigital interfaces, BCI, cognitive networking, adaptive systems, neuro-rights, human-AI interaction
